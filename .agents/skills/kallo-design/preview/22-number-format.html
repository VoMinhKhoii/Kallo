DON'T — fake precision
585.4 kcal
68.2735 kg · 12,847 steps · 1.2847 km
Decimals where they don't exist. Estimated kcal isn't a real to-tenths number.
DO — bounded & rounded
585 kcal (range 560–610)
68.5 kg · 12,800 steps · 1.3 km
Integer for estimated kcal, one decimal for weight, round step counts. Bounded ranges when shown.
DON'T — ambiguous units
P 22
C 50
F 28
grams? percent? of what?
Letter-then-number without unit reads as code, not content.
DO — spell the unit
Protein38 / 140 g
Carbs125 / 250 g
Fat22 / 70 g
`current / target g` — current bold, target light, unit lowercase. Always.
DON'T — robot dates
2026-05-15 / 12:47:00
Last logged: 2026-05-14T20:10:00Z
ISO format leaks the implementation. Reads as a log line.
DO — written dates
Today — May 15, 2026
Last meal: yesterday at 8:10 pm
Relative on the dashboard, absolute beside it. Lowercase am/pm. The em-dash is the Kallo divider.
DON'T — punctuation soup
3 days, 84% confidence | 30 days view
Comma + pipe = email-signature aesthetic.
DO — middle-dot separators
3 logged days · 84% avg confidence · 30 days view
U+00B7 middle dot (·) with one space each side. The "trust line" pattern.
