This is Kallo's main way in. You write a sentence; Kallo returns dishes,
ingredients, weights and nutrition for you to check and confirm.

## How to write

There is no required syntax. But a few habits make results noticeably better.

### Keep Vietnamese diacritics

`bò`, `bơ` and `bổ` are three different things. Kallo relies on the marks to
tell them apart, so writing without them degrades results significantly.

### Say how it was cooked

Cooking method moves fat more than almost anything else:

- `cá kho` and `cá chiên` — same fish, clearly different.
- `rau luộc` and `rau xào` — the difference is the oil.
- `gà nướng` and `gà rán` — a large gap.

### Give weights when you know them

A specific number always beats a guess. `50gr nạc dăm luộc` is used as written,
with no estimation applied.

### Write the whole meal in one go

`cơm tấm sườn bì chả + canh chua + 1 ly trà đá` gives better results than three
separate entries, because Kallo sees the whole meal at once and groups the
dishes correctly.

## What happens when you send

Results stream in rather than appearing all at once. You will see the stages
follow each other:

<Steps>
<Step index={1} title="Breaking down the meal">
Your sentence is separated into dishes, each into ingredients, with cooking
methods and any portion hints you gave. No grams are produced at this point.
</Step>

<Step index={2} title="Matching ingredients">
Each ingredient is looked up in the food composition data. Dish names appear
first at this stage.
</Step>

<Step index={3} title="Anchoring the portion">
The weight gets settled. If you stated one, Kallo uses exactly that. If the dish
is too ambiguous to settle, this step becomes a question for you.
</Step>

<Step index={4} title="Estimating nutrition">
For each matched ingredient, nutrition is computed with bounds, then collapsed
to the figure shown.
</Step>
</Steps>

## When Kallo asks instead

Sometimes you get a question rather than a number. That is intentional.

`1 tô canh` could be 200ml or 700ml — too wide a gap to guess. Rather than
quietly picking something in the middle, Kallo asks which bowl you used, and
offers reference sizes with pictures to choose from.

<Callout label="Why ask rather than guess">
A confident wrong estimate is worse than a question. Kallo has an explicit
threshold for this: when the plausible weight range is too wide, it stops and
asks.
</Callout>

## Adjusting before you save

Tap **Edit** to nudge each item. For ingredients measured in grams or millilitres
each tap moves 10 units; for countable things, one unit per tap. Totals update
live.

Dishes served in a vessel — bowls, cups, plates — also get a size picker with
reference photographs.

Nothing is saved until you confirm.

## When the result is wrong

A few options, in the order worth trying:

1. **Rewrite more specifically.** Add the cooking method, add a weight, split
   the dishes out.
2. **Adjust by hand after analysis.** Faster than rewriting if only one item is
   off.
3. **Use [manual logging](/docs/logging/manual)** if you weighed the
   ingredients.
4. **Send feedback.** If an ingredient is missing or matched wrongly, tell us in
   Settings — it goes straight to the team.
