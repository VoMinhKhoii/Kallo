---
title: Log your first meal
description: From a sentence to a saved meal, in about a minute.
lastUpdated: '2026-08-03'
---

You do not need to finish your profile to try this. Kallo uses defaults until
you tell it more.

<Steps>
<Step index={1} title="Open Logging">
Pick **Logging** in the sidebar. The input sits at the bottom of the screen,
like a chat box.
</Step>

<Step index={2} title="Describe the meal">
Write it the way you would tell someone. `2 mực kho mặn + 50gr nạc dăm luộc +
1 chén cơm` works as well as `bún chả Hà Nội`. Keep Vietnamese diacritics — they
carry real meaning, and Kallo relies on them.
</Step>

<Step index={3} title="Watch it break the meal down">
Results arrive progressively: dish names first, then the numbers. If a portion
is ambiguous, Kallo asks you — which bowl you used, for instance — rather than
guessing.
</Step>

<Step index={4} title="Adjust if needed">
Tap **Edit** to nudge any item's amount up or down. Totals update as you go.
</Step>

<Step index={5} title="Confirm">
Tap confirm to save. The meal enters today's log and your
[dashboard](/docs/tracking/dashboard) updates immediately.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout label="Writing a good description" tone="tip">
The more specific you are about cooking method, the closer the estimate lands.
"Braised fish" and "fried fish" are the same fish with meaningfully different
fat. If you know a weight, just write it — `50gr nạc dăm` beats any guess.
</Callout>

## Nothing is saved yet

An analysis is not in your log until you confirm it. If the result is well off,
you can discard it and rewrite more clearly — nothing gets recorded.
