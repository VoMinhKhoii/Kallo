## 1. What Kallo does, and what it does not

Kallo helps you log meals in natural language and **estimate** their nutrition.
It breaks your sentence into dishes and ingredients, matches them against food
composition data (FAO, USDA, and the Vietnam National Food Composition Table
2007), and returns weights and nutrition figures with bounds.

Three limits you should understand before relying on a number:

- **These are estimates, not measurements.** Results depend on how you describe
  a meal, how it was actually cooked, and how well the reference data covers it.
  Error is normal and expected.
- **Kallo is not a medical device.** It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any
  condition, and it is not a substitute for advice from a doctor, dietitian or
  other qualified health professional.
- **Decisions remain yours.** If you are pregnant, managing a medical
  condition, recovering from an eating disorder, or following a treatment plan,
  talk to a health professional before changing your diet based on figures in
  the app.
