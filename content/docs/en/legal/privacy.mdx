shares and protects personal data when you use Kallo on the web, iOS and
Android.

It applies to every Kallo account, including accounts without a Premium
subscription, and to anyone who joins the waitlist before having an account.

## 2. What we collect

### Account information

Your email address, sign-in method (email and password, Google, or Apple), and
an account identifier. We do **not** store readable passwords — authentication
is handled by Supabase Auth.

### Profile and goals

Display name, handle, avatar, the language you picked; weight, height, age,
biological sex and activity level; your goal of cutting, bulking or maintaining,
the pace you set for it, and the calorie and macro targets derived from those;
the country you grew up in and the country you live in.

Both countries are ones **you pick yourself** during onboarding, from a list.
Kallo does **not** read your device location: the app requests no location
permission, uses no GPS, and does not infer where you are from your IP address.

### Cooking habits

How much oil you typically use, your default rice and protein portions, whether
you add sugar to braised dishes, and how much broth you drink. These make
estimates match how you actually cook.

### Meal log

The sentence you write to describe a meal, when you logged it, the meal slot,
the entry mode (Normal, Manual or Cheat meal), the dishes and ingredients Kallo
recognises, estimated weights, cooking method, ingredient-match confidence,
alcohol where relevant, the slider positions when you use Cheat meal, and all 28
nutrient values computed for each meal and each item.

### Weight over time

Your weight entries and the dates they belong to.

### Circle content

Friendships, meals you share, reactions, replies, portion invites, the groups
you join and the messages you send in groups or private chats.

### Feedback

Feedback type, your message, any screenshot you attach (stored in a private,
non-public bucket), plus app version, platform, language and the screen you were
on.

### Billing

Premium entitlement status, the store you purchased through, product identifier,
start and expiry, renewal state, and the customer identifier issued by the
billing provider. **We never receive or store full card numbers** — payment
details are handled by Apple, Google, RevenueCat Billing or Stripe.

### Waitlist

If you sign up before having an account: email, language, signup source, a
hashed confirmation token, and a **hash of your IP address** used for abuse
prevention. We do not retain the raw IP address for this purpose.

### Operational and anti-abuse data

Technical records of each analysis: processing stages, timings, token counts and
errors. Anti-abuse events are recorded against **HMAC-hashed identifiers**
rather than raw account identifiers.

## 3. What actually reaches the AI model

When you request a meal analysis, the AI model receives **only**:

- Your meal description.
- The country you grew up in and the country you live in.
- Your cooking habits.
- The language you want the result in.

The model does **not** know your weight goal, your pace, your calorie or
macro targets, weight, height, age, sex, email, name, or meal history.

<Callout label="Why this matters" tone="tip">
Goal adjustment happens **on our servers, after** the model returns — not by
telling the model you are cutting. That keeps your goal from biasing the
estimate, and the boundary is enforced by the type system in our source code
rather than by convention.
</Callout>

We do **not** use your content to train shared AI models.

### What happens to it on Google's side

Production Kallo calls Gemini through **Vertex AI on Google Cloud** — the
enterprise service, not the consumer Gemini app. Under Google Cloud's terms,
prompts and responses are **not used to train or fine-tune Google's models**.

There are two exceptions:

- **Caching for up to 24 hours.** Google caches inputs to reduce latency on
  subsequent requests. The cached copy sits in the data centre that served the
  request.
- **Abuse monitoring for up to 30 days.** If automated safety classifiers flag a
  request as suspicious, Google may retain that prompt to investigate a policy
  violation. That data is still **not** used to train models.

Kallo calls Vertex AI's **global endpoint**, so a request may be served from any
region Google operates in. That means your meal description — and only the meal
description plus the four fields above — can leave Vietnam while it is being
processed. See section 9.

Developer machines and internal scripts use a different connection (Google AI
Studio) with test data, not your account data.

## 6. When you share in Circle

A meal you share is visible to the audience you choose — friends, a group, or a
private chat — including its description, dishes and nutrition figures.

Friend invites work by link, so anyone holding the link can send a request. You
can remove a friend at any time. If you enable automatic sharing in Settings,
new meals are shared without further action — check that setting if you want
your log to stay private.

Content others have already viewed or copied into their own log may persist
after you delete your original.

## 7. Retention and deletion

We keep account content while your account is active, and afterwards only for as
long as needed for security, dispute resolution, legal compliance and backup
recovery.

You can delete your account in **Settings**. Deletion runs as a scheduled job
that also erases your customer record at the billing provider.

<Callout label="Before you delete" tone="caution">
Deleting your Kallo account does **not** cancel a subscription bought through
Apple or Google. Cancel in the store first if you want charges to stop. A
subscription bought on the web is cancelled as part of deletion.
</Callout>

Deletion removes your data from the systems that serve the app. Where our
hosting provider holds system-level backups, deleted data may persist in them
until they are overwritten; during that window it is not used to serve the app
and is not accessible in it. We do not currently publish a fixed backup
retention period, and we will state one here once we set it.

## 8. Security

We apply several layers of protection:

- Encrypted connections in transit.
- Row-level authorisation in the database, so one account's data cannot be read
  from another.
- Server-side permission checks on every cross-account read in Circle.
- An origin lock that only accepts traffic arriving through our edge layer.
- Private storage buckets for avatars and feedback screenshots.
- Tightly restricted access to server credentials.

No system is perfectly secure. If an incident affects your personal data, we
will notify you as applicable law requires.

## 9. International transfers

The providers listed in section 5 may process data in countries other than where
you live. Specifically:

- **Application servers** run on Google Cloud Run in the `asia-southeast3`
  region (Bangkok, Thailand). Google Cloud has no region in Vietnam, so
  application data is processed outside Vietnam.
- **AI meal analysis** goes through Vertex AI's global endpoint, so it may be
  processed in any region Google operates in, including outside Vietnam. Only
  the four fields listed in section 3 travel this path.
- **Every other provider** processes data in the regions it operates.

Where data leaves Vietnam, we do so in line with personal data protection law,
including preparing and retaining a transfer impact assessment where required.

## 10. Your rights

Under Decree 13/2023/ND-CP on personal data protection, and under the law where
you live, you have the right to:

- **Know** what data is processed and how.
- **Access** and receive a copy of your data.
- **Correct** inaccurate data.
- **Withdraw consent** you previously gave.
- **Delete** your personal data.
- **Restrict** or **object to** certain processing.
- **Receive your data** in a portable format.
- **Complain** to the competent authority.
- **Claim compensation** for damage, as the law provides.

To exercise any of these, email
[support@kallo.fit](mailto:support@kallo.fit). We may need to verify your
identity first, and will respond within the statutory period.

Most requests you can handle yourself immediately: edit your profile, delete an
individual meal, or delete your whole account — all in **Settings**.

If you believe your child has given us data without the necessary consent,

## 12. Cookies and on-device storage

We use cookies and local storage only as far as necessary:

- Keeping you signed in.
- Remembering your chosen language.
- Remembering interface state, such as whether the sidebar is open or collapsed.

We use no advertising cookies and embed no third-party ad-network trackers.

## 13. Automated decision-making

Kallo's nutrition estimates are produced by automated means. Those estimates do
**not** constitute automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant
effects within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR: they are nutritional information,
and they determine no access to services, credit, employment or any other
entitlement.

You can review, edit or delete any individual result both before and after it is
saved. If you would like a human to review how a result was produced, contact
[support@kallo.fit](mailto:support@kallo.fit) and we will explain the main
factors involved.

We do **not** carry out profiling for advertising, credit scoring, insurance
risk assessment, or any purpose beyond delivering the service you requested.

## 14. Do-Not-Track signals

Most web browsers, and some mobile operating systems and applications, include a
Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature you can use to signal a preference not to have your
browsing activity monitored.

No uniform technology standard for recognising and honouring DNT signals has
been finalised. We therefore **do not respond** to DNT signals or any comparable
automated mechanism. Should a binding standard be adopted, we will update this
policy. California law requires us to disclose this.

information.

1798.83, California residents may request, once a year and free of charge,
information about the categories of personal data (if any) we disclosed to third
parties for their direct marketing purposes. Because we make no such
disclosures, the answer to any such request is "none".
