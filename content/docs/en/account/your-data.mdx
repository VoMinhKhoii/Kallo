This is the readable summary. The full, legally operative version is the
[privacy policy](/docs/legal/privacy).

## What Kallo stores

- **Account** — your email and how you sign in.
- **Profile** — display name, avatar, language, countries.
- **Body metrics** — weight, height, age, sex, activity, goal and targets.
- **Cooking habits** — oil, default portions, broth.
- **Meal log** — the sentence you wrote, the dishes, weights and 28 nutrient
  values.
- **Nutrition label photos** — each label you scan, in private storage.
- **Weight over time.**
- **Circle content** — friends, shared meals, messages.
- **Feedback** — what you sent and any screenshot.
- **Billing status** — never a card number.

## What actually reaches the AI model

This is the question most worth asking, and the answer is more specific than you
might expect.

When you request a meal analysis, the model receives only:

- Your meal description
- Your country of origin and country of residence
- Your cooking habits
- The language you want the result in

When you scan a nutrition label, it receives only the photo. Kallo also keeps
that photo privately, visible only to you and the Kallo team, until you delete
your account. Ingredient search text may also go to Google's embedding model to
find similar ingredients. Kallo asks your permission before your first AI
analysis or label scan.

The model does **not** receive: your weight, height, age, sex, goal, pace,
calorie targets, email, name, or meal history.

<Callout label="Why it is separated like this" tone="tip">
Two reasons. The first is privacy: the model does not need to know your weight
to work out what is in a bowl of noodle soup.

The second is estimate quality. A model that knows you are cutting is very
likely to return a comfortable number rather than a true one. Goal adjustment
happens on our servers **after** the model returns, so the underlying estimate is
never bent toward your goal.
</Callout>

Your content is **not** used to train shared AI models. Kallo calls Gemini
through Vertex AI on Google Cloud, where the enterprise terms say prompts and
responses are not used to train Google's models. Google does cache inputs for up
to 24 hours to reduce latency, and may retain a request for up to 30 days if a
safety classifier flags it as suspicious — detail in the
[privacy policy](/docs/legal/privacy).

## Getting your data out

**Settings → Account → Export my data** downloads a JSON file straight away, on
the web and in the mobile app. It contains:

- **Account** — your id, email, when you signed up and last signed in, and your
  sign-in methods: each linked Google, Apple or email identity with its dates,
  plus the profile details it holds (name, photo link, email and whether it is
  verified). Sign-in tokens are never included.
- **Profile** — body metrics, goal and targets, countries, language, cooking
  habits, onboarding progress, and whether new meals are shared to your Circle
  automatically.
- **Circle profile** — your handle, display name and avatar settings.
- **Meal log** — every meal with its dishes and nutrients, your weight history,
  and the days you marked as fully logged.
- **Circle** — your friends (by handle, with when each friendship was
  accepted), the meals you shared, the reactions and replies you left, meal
  copies and splits you sent or received, and your own Circle activity.
- **Chats** — the chats you are in or created, who is in them (by user id), and
  every message you sent.
- **Notifications** and the devices registered for push notifications. Device
  tokens are cut down to their last few characters.
- **Feedback** you sent, with its status.
- **Billing** — your Premium grants and their sync state.
- **App activity** — meal-analysis requests still held (kept for about a week),
  analyses not yet saved, ingredients the food database could not match, and
  product analytics events linked to your account.
- **Files** — your uploaded profile photo and any feedback screenshots, listed
  by storage location, and your label photos, listed by identifier, date and
  linked meal. The images themselves are not inside the file.

Other people's content stays out: the export has your own messages, replies and
reactions, not theirs. Friendships you have blocked are left out too, because
the record does not show who blocked whom. Internal records such as rate-limit
counters, model-debugging traces and raw billing-provider webhooks are not
included. For anything not in the file, including a copy of a screenshot, write
to [support@kallo.fit](mailto:support@kallo.fit).

## Deleting data

- **A single meal** — delete it directly in your log.
- **Your whole account** — in **Settings**. Deletion runs as a scheduled job,
  which also erases your customer record at the billing provider.

<Callout label="Cancel before you delete" tone="caution">
Deleting your account does **not** cancel a subscription bought through Apple or
Google. Cancel in the store first. See [Kallo Premium](/docs/account/premium).
</Callout>

Backups rotate on a cycle, so deleted data can sit in a backup briefly before
being overwritten. During that window it is no longer used to serve the app.

## Your rights

You have the right to know, access, correct, delete, restrict processing,
withdraw consent, receive your data in a portable form, and complain to the
competent authority. The full list and how to exercise it is in the
[privacy policy](/docs/legal/privacy).
