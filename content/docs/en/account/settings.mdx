Settings is a single page, divided into sections you can jump to with the
in-page navigation.

## Profile

Display name and avatar — this is what friends in
[Circle](/docs/circle/friends-and-feed) see. Avatars are kept in private
storage, not a public bucket.

## Body metrics and goal

Weight, height, age, biological sex, activity level, goal and the aggression
slider.

When you change something that affects your targets, the save button shows the
new target before you commit — you see the consequence before accepting it. If
the pace you picked is a sharp one, Kallo says so.

More in [Profile and targets](/docs/profile-and-targets).

## Region and language

Country of origin, country of residence, and app language. The first two shape
how Kallo reads dish names and default portions, so it is worth keeping them
accurate.

## Cooking habits

Oil use, default rice and protein portions, sugar in braised dishes, whether you
finish the broth. If estimates are consistently off in the same direction, this
is the first place to look.

## Sharing

Turn automatic sharing to Circle on or off. See
[Sharing and splits](/docs/circle/sharing-and-splits).

## Subscription

Your current Premium status and the plan management link. Detail in
[Kallo Premium](/docs/account/premium).

## Feedback

Report a bug, flag a missing ingredient, or suggest an idea — with a screenshot
if it helps. Feedback goes straight to the team, tagged with your app version
and the screen you were on so we can reproduce it.

If an ingredient is missing or matched wrongly, this is the fastest route to
getting it fixed.

## Account

Change your password, sign out, and delete your account.

<Callout label="Before deleting your account" tone="caution">
Deleting your Kallo account does **not** cancel a subscription bought through
Apple or Google. Cancel in the store first if you want charges to stop. See
[Your data](/docs/account/your-data).
</Callout>
