## Sharing a meal

Once saved, every meal has a share action. You pick the audience: friends, a
group, or a private chat.

Recipients see the description you wrote, the dishes, and the nutrition figures.

### Automatic sharing

**Settings** has an option to share new meals to Circle automatically. It is
**off** until you turn it on — a new account keeps every meal private.

<Callout label="What turning it on means" tone="caution">
While it is on, every new meal is shared with your friends as you save it,
without asking again. Each meal still has its own share action, so you can make
any one of them private afterwards.
</Callout>

A friend sees only meals shared **after** you connected. If you had automatic
sharing on before adding someone, your earlier meals do not appear in their
feed.

## Splits

This is the part for eating together.

If two people share one bowl and both log the whole bowl, the numbers are wrong
twice over. Portion invites fix that.

When sharing, you choose one of two modes:

**Copy** — the recipient logs the **whole** meal. Use it when you both had the
same thing.

**Split** — the recipient logs a **fraction** of the meal, at the ratio you set.
Use it when you genuinely shared one portion.

The recipient sees the invite and decides whether to accept. Nobody gets a meal
added to their diary without agreeing to it.

## Reactions and replies

Shared meals can take reactions and short replies. This is the light-touch part
of the feature — enough to keep a habit going without turning eating into a
competition.

## Removing something you shared

You can stop sharing a meal. It then disappears from other people's threads.

But if someone has **already logged it into their own diary**, that entry is
theirs and it stays — much as you cannot take back food you have already handed
to someone.
