Circle is the social side of Kallo. It is deliberately small and private — no
discovery page, no leaderboards, no strangers.

## Adding friends

There is no username search. You connect by **sharing a link**.

<Steps>
<Step index={1} title="Create an invite link">
Open the friends area and generate an invite link.
</Step>

<Step index={2} title="Send it to whoever you want">
By message, wherever you already talk.
</Step>

<Step index={3} title="They open it and tap Accept">
That tap connects you straight away — there is no second approval step on your
side. You get a notification that they joined.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout label="Links can be forwarded" tone="caution">
Anyone holding the link can use it to connect with you — including someone you
did not mean to invite, if the link gets passed on. The connection is made the
moment they accept, so share the link only with people you want in your Circle.
If the wrong person joins, remove them; they lose access to what you share from
then on.
</Callout>

Having no user-search page is a deliberate choice: it means nobody can find your
account from your display name.

## The feed

The feed shows meals your friends have shared, newest first. For each one you
can:

- **React** — a way to say "that looks good" without typing.
- **Reply** — a short comment in the thread.
- **Log it to your own diary** — useful when you both ate at the same place.

Only shared meals appear. Your log is private by default, and a new friend sees
only what you share **after** you connect — meals you shared before that stay
out of their feed.

## Who sees what

You decide per share. Automatic sharing is off until you turn it on in
Settings; once on, every new meal is shared with your friends as you save it.
When a meal is shared, everyone in its audience sees its description, its dishes
and its nutrition figures. A friend sees the meals you shared from the moment
you connected onwards, not your earlier history.

The detail on audiences and splitting is in
[Sharing and splits](/docs/circle/sharing-and-splits).

## Removing a friend

You can do this at any time. Afterwards they no longer see what you share. What
they already saw, or already copied into their own log, stays on their side.
