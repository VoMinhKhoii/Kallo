There is no phone queue and no ticket portal. Email works, and it is read by
the founder.

## Support

**[support@kallo.fit](mailto:support@kallo.fit)**

For anything about using Kallo: an estimate that came out wrong, a meal that
would not save, a question about your plan, a refund, or a feature you wish
existed. Written in English or Vietnamese — both are answered in the language
you wrote in.

Expect a reply within a couple of working days. If you are reporting something
broken, the two things that help most are what you typed and roughly when, so
the request can be found.

## In the app

Settings has a feedback form that attaches your app version and, if you choose,
a screenshot. That is the fastest route for a bug, because it arrives with the
context already attached. It reaches the same inbox as the support address.

## Security

**[security@kallo.fit](mailto:security@kallo.fit)**

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately here — not in a public issue,
discussion or pull request. Include what you observed, how to reproduce it, and
whether you want credit in the changelog after a fix ships.

The commitments, in full, are in
[SECURITY.md](https://github.com/VoMinhKhoii/Kallo/blob/main/SECURITY.md):
acknowledgement within 48 hours, triage and a severity assessment within five
working days, and a coordinated disclosure plan once a fix is ready.

## Privacy and your data

For access, correction, export or deletion requests, write to
[support@kallo.fit](mailto:support@kallo.fit). Most of it you can do yourself
without asking: [your data](/docs/account/your-data) explains what the
self-serve export includes and how to delete the account outright.

The data controller and the registered address are named in the
[privacy policy](/docs/legal/privacy).

## Developers and AI agents

[The API page](/docs/developers/api) covers authentication, errors and what is
safe to build on, and [Kallo for AI agents](/docs/developers/agents) covers when
Kallo is the right tool. If you want something the API does not do yet, the
support address is the right place to say so — there is no separate developer
queue, and there does not need to be one at this size.

## Partnerships and press

**[founder@kallo.fit](mailto:founder@kallo.fit)**

Partnerships, press, investors and commercial licensing go straight to the
founder. For press, say what you are writing and by when.
