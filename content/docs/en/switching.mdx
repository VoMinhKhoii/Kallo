Kallo has no import from MyFitnessPal, Cal AI or any other app. Your old diary,
saved foods and weight history stay in the app you used. You start a fresh log,
and three things are worth entering by hand on the first day.

## What to enter by hand

- Your body metrics and goal. Weight, height, age, sex, activity and goal set
  your daily targets, and setup takes about two minutes. See
  [Profile and targets](/docs/profile-and-targets).
- Your cooking habits, from the same setup: how much oil you cook with, your
  usual rice and protein portions, and whether you finish the broth. Kallo uses
  them on every meal you type.
- Today's weight, from the quick entry on the dashboard, so the trend line has a
  first point. See [Weight](/docs/tracking/weight).

## What changes day to day

### You type the meal

You stop searching a database for each food or taking a photo of the plate.
You write the meal the way you would say it:

`two scrambled eggs with a bit of butter, one slice of sourdough, half an avocado`

Kallo splits that into foods, matches each one against food composition data,
and shows calories and macros for you to check before you save. Say how it was
cooked and give a weight when you know one. When the words leave a portion
open, Kallo asks which bowl or cup you used. More in
[Describing a meal](/docs/logging/describe-a-meal).

### Packaged food still scans

[Barcode scanning](/docs/logging/barcode) works for packaged food. When the
barcode is not in the database, [scan the nutrition label](/docs/logging/nutrition-label)
on the pack instead.

### Each figure comes from a range

Every app that gives one number for a meal is estimating. Kallo works out a
plausible range for each meal and saves one figure from inside it. Portion size
and cooking oil move that range the most. [How estimates work](/docs/estimates/how-it-works)
says where the numbers come from, and [Accuracy and limits](/docs/estimates/accuracy-and-limits)
says how far off a single day can be.

## Your first week

<Steps>
<Step index={1} title="Log every meal, even roughly">
A day with a missing meal is further off than a day of rough guesses.
</Step>

<Step index={2} title="Relog the meals you repeat">
Type `/` and pick a dish you logged before. It keeps the numbers you confirmed
last time, so the same breakfast reads the same every day. See
[Re-logging past meals](/docs/logging/relog).
</Step>

<Step index={3} title="Check one result against a label">
Type a packaged food you have the label for, as you would describe it, then
compare Kallo's figure with the label's.
</Step>

<Step index={4} title="Weigh in the same way each morning">
Watch the trend line over the weeks, and let a single heavy morning go.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Plans and platforms

Kallo is free while it is in beta. After beta, typing a meal is part of
[Premium](/docs/account/premium), at $29.99 a year on the web. Barcode scanning
and manual logging stay on the free plan.

Kallo runs on the web now. An iPhone app is coming. There is no Android app.
