# Kallo logo exploration

## Approved mark (2026-07-16)

The segmented K is approved as the Kallo symbol. Final geometry, all derived
from the option B wordmark's capital K with its junction hidden:

- Three components: the untouched 168×708 stem, plus the arm and leg resolved
  as perfect parallelograms. Cap/baseline tip edges and stroke widths are the
  original letterform's; the diagonals pivot slightly on the fixed tips
  (arm ≈50.6°, leg ≈53.4°, vs the font's 46.9°/49.8°).
- Spacing derives from the stroke: 28-unit slit beside the stem (stem ÷ 6) and
  a 112-unit void between arm and leg (4 × 28 = ⅔ stem), a 1:4 rhythm.
- Files: `refinements/kallo-mark-segmented-k.svg` (symbol, 656×708),
  `exports/kallo-wordmark-segmented.svg` (wordmark with the segmented K as its
  capital — the K is never doubled next to the wordmark), and
  `refinements/segmented-k-preview.html` (approval board).

Everything below this section is the historical study that led here.

This folder contains the pre-selection logo study for Kallo. None of these
concepts are production assets. Production rollout remains gated on an explicit
finalist selection.

The user locked concept 01's segmented K as the symbol. `round-two.html` holds
that mark constant while comparing typography voices derived from concepts 03,
05, 06, and 08. The lettering remains live reference type until one voice is
selected and redrawn as custom SVG paths.

Option B is the selected typography direction. `round-three.html` compares the
original segmented K with a matched construction: the capital K stem from option
B's live wordmark plus two vertically mirrored, perfect parallelogram arms.
Production path conversion remains gated on approval of that three-part geometry.

The selected option B lettering is also available independently at
`exports/kallo-option-b-wordmark.svg`. It is an Avenir Next Bold outline with the
approved `-0.06em` tracking, exported as one `currentColor` path with no live text
or font dependency. This wordmark export does not approve or roll out an app icon.

## Creative brief

- Brand: Kallo, pronounced like “calo” in “calories”.
- Audience: experienced calorie and macro trackers who can sanity-check an AI
  estimate and want less friction than a spreadsheet.
- Promise: transparent intelligence and lighter logging, without implying that
  fitness progress itself has a shortcut.
- Priority: a memorable, ownable mobile app icon.
- Palette: cream `#FEFBF6`, espresso `#2C2416`, tan `#C9A87C`, and umber
  `#695E4E`.
- Exclusions: literal food, fitness, camera, and generic AI symbols.

## Reference territories

The comparison board frames every concept against three premium territories:

1. **Quiet precision** — exact spacing, calm contrast, instrument-like clarity.
2. **Editorial craft** — warm paper, typographic confidence, tactile restraint.
3. **Performance object** — strong silhouette, controlled mass, no gym clichés.

## Selection method

Choose two or three directions by number. Feedback should identify what to keep
and what to change, for example: “Keep 02’s silhouette, soften the inner cut,” or
“Explore 07 with the weight and confidence of 06.” The next round will make
targeted refinements only.

## Collision screen

Screened on 2026-07-13 against the current public identities and app-icon
fields for [MacroFactor](https://macrofactor.com/),
[MyFitnessPal](https://www.myfitnesspal.com/),
[Cal AI](https://www.calai.app/), and
[Foodvisor](https://www.foodvisor.io/en/), plus MacroFactor's published
[food-logger icon comparison](https://macrofactorapp.com/fastest-food-logger/).

The field is crowded with literal food and body imagery, circular gauges,
camera frames, bright health greens, and bold athletic initials. These eight
directions avoid those category defaults. Concepts 01, 02, and 07 still use a
K initial, so their final geometry would need the most careful similarity and
trademark review if shortlisted. Concepts 04, 06, and 08 create more unusual
name- or promise-specific territory.

This is an informal visual collision screen, not legal or trademark clearance.
