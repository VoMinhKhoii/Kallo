Kallo identity study · round one

Eight ways to make tracking feel lighter.

Kallo is a premium calorie-tracking instrument for people who already understand the numbers. These are deliberately different ideas, held to the same mobile-icon and 24-pixel tests. No food symbols, fitness tropes, camera metaphors, or generic AI effects.

Territory 01

Quiet precision

Measured spacing and calm contrast. A dependable instrument, not a dashboard shouting for attention.

Territory 02

Editorial craft

Warm paper and typographic confidence. Human enough to live with every day, disciplined enough to trust.

Territory 03

Performance object

Strong mass and a memorable silhouette. Premium training equipment without aggressive gym language.
