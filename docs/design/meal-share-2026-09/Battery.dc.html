The portion control, battery style

Four readings of “battery”, drawn at working size. The shell is always the whole dish — it is never half-empty, because someone ate all of it. What changes between variants is how the division is expressed: continuous versus stepped, and whether the numbers live inside the meter or under it.

Four readings
V1
Capsule · continuous
Bạn 65% · 676 kcalMai 35% · 364 kcal

The battery silhouette, nothing inside it. Terminal nub on the right, 2pt ink shell, two fills meeting at a white grip. Every number lives under the meter where it can use real type sizes. Drags continuously, snaps to 50%.
Reads as: a charge level. Cleanest, quietest, most Kallo.

V2
Cells · ten steps
Bạn 60% · 624 kcalMai 40% · 416 kcal

Ten cells, one tick each. The step IS the control: no fiddly pixel-hunting, a selectionClick per cell, and a tap on any cell jumps straight there. 10% granularity, which is honestly all anyone can judge about a plate of food.
Reads as: a battery gauge you fill. Most tactile, most forgiving of fat thumbs.

V3
Cells · twenty steps
Bạn 65% · 676 kcalMai 35% · 364 kcal

Same idea at 5% resolution. Finer control, but the cells get thin enough that the meter starts to read as texture rather than countable units — and nobody eats 65% of a bánh mì on purpose.
Reads as: an equalizer. Precision we cannot actually justify.

The end stops. Zero on either side is not a split, it is a transfer — and the server rejects it (0.1–0.9). The meter refuses to go there rather than erroring after the fact.

Where this lands
1

V2 at ten cells is the recommendation. The step is the control: no pixel-hunting, a haptic per cell, tap-to-jump, and 10% is the honest limit of what anyone can judge about a plate of food. It is also the only variant where the division stays countable.

2

V1 is the fallback if it reads too loud. Same anatomy, continuous fill, snap at 50%. Swapping between them is a one-line change in the widget, not a redesign — both take the same (myShare, onChanged, interactive) API.

3

The nub carries the metaphor. Drop it and V1 is just a progress bar; keep it and the control announces “this is a level you set”. It costs 5pt of trailing width and appears in every state, including read-only.

4

Accessibility is unchanged from the slider plan. Semantics(slider:) with onIncrease/onDecrease stepping one cell, value spoken as “Bạn 60 phần trăm, Mai 40 phần trăm”. Read-only instances drop the slider role and expose a label only.
