Share a meal — how native do we go

Four directions for the sender sheet, ordered by how much they defer to iOS convention over Kallo's own chrome. Every one of them drops the chips, pins a full-width action, and kills the two-card mode grid. Pick one — or mix: the header, the portion control and the list style are independent choices.

The iOS action-sheet shape: hairline-divided full-bleed rows, no button chrome at all. Calmest of the three — but it reads as a menu, which undersells “accept this into your diary”.

The rules behind all four
1

One full-width primary per surface, at the bottom. 52pt, pinned, thumb-reachable. Secondary actions are text, not outlined pills. Nothing important is ever a 34pt chip in a corner.

2

Selection is a trailing checkmark, not a wash. The beige row fill reads as hover, which touch does not have. iOS marks the chosen row at the trailing edge and leaves the background alone.

3

Separators, not cards. Four friends in four bordered cards is a web list. One hairline inset past the avatar is the iOS list, and it lets the rows breathe at 50pt.

4

Explanations go in footers. iOS has a slot for “what this will do”: 13pt muted, under the group it describes. That is where the rescale warning belongs — not in a banner, not in an alert.

5

Haptics are part of the visual design. selectionClick on every friend toggle and mode change, lightImpact when the portion bar snaps to 50%. Silence is what makes a Flutter sheet feel like a web page.

6

Never invent a control the platform already has — and never ship two controls for one concept. Directions A and C both honour this (stock slider / the app’s own ruler); B and D earn their custom bar only if two-sided numbers matter.

7

Titles go left, at 28/600. A centred 16pt sheet title is an Android/web habit. iOS sheets that matter use a large left-aligned title; the centred one is for utility sheets with no content of their own.

8

Chip inventory to delete: two mode cards, the beige selected-row wash, the 1/2 phần portion pill, the two invite action pills, and the accept/dismiss pills in the web activity row. Five shapes, all replaced by rows, checkmarks, geometry or full-width buttons.
